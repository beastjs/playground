## [0.3.1] - 2026-09-07

### Changed

- Updated the published toolchain references to `beast-tsrx@0.2.60`, `create-beast@0.2.60`, and `octane@0.2.6`.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-08-29

### Added

- Added authoring guidance for setup-bearing and code-only `scope` children.
- Added Vite, Rspack, and Rsbuild guidance for Octane's experimental
  `nativeReads` compiler option.

### Changed

- Updated compatibility and coverage guidance for `beast-tsrx@0.2.9`,
  `octane@0.1.49`, and the `0.1.44` Octane Rspack/Rsbuild plugins.
- Expanded Strong-mode coverage notes to include Effect Event and
  nondeterministic-render diagnostics.
- Documented the project-owned changelog generated by `create-beast@0.2.12`.
